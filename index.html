Quick Tips

Water: Always carry 2L minimum. Fountains marked on the map are potable unless noted.
Camping: Wild camping ("bivouac") is tolerated in many areas if you pitch after 7pm and leave by 9am. National parks are stricter.
Food Budget: Expect to spend €10-15/day buying from supermarkets and markets. Baguette + cheese + tomatoes = classic cyclist lunch.
Season: Best months are May-June and September. July-August is hot (35°C+) and crowded.
Mistral: The strong north wind can blow for days. Plan routes south-to-north if possible to ride with it.

About Provence by Bike

This site is for self-supported bike tourists planning multi-day rides through Provence with their own bike, tent, and camping stove.

What You'll Find

  • 5 pre-planned routes from 3 to 5 days, covering easy coastal rides to the Mont Ventoux challenge.
  • Camping spots — both municipal campsites (€10-15/night) and free bivouac locations.
  • Cheap food sources — supermarkets (Lidl, Intermarché), weekly markets with local produce, and village bakeries.
  • Water fountains — potable water refill points along each route.
  • Bike shops — for repairs and spare parts.
  • Attractions — Roman ruins, lavender fields, gorges, and the prettiest villages.

Packing Essentials

  • 2L water capacity minimum (3L in summer)
  • Camping stove + fuel canister (buy Campingaz at Decathlon or large supermarkets)
  • Lightweight tent — summer nights are warm, 3-season is fine
  • Basic bike repair kit: spare tube, pump, multitool, tire levers, chain link
  • Sun protection: hat, sunscreen, arm sleeves
  • Rain jacket (rare in summer but the Mistral brings cold snaps)

Budget

Self-supported touring in Provence can be done for €20-30/day including camping fees, food from supermarkets/markets, and the occasional café stop. Cooking your own meals with a stove brings costs down further.

Getting There

TGV trains serve Avignon, Aix-en-Provence, and Marseille. Bikes travel free on TGV if you book a bike space (limited, book early). Regional TER trains accept bikes without reservation.
